## Research. Build. Share.

REC OpenWorks makes useful engineering work easier to find, understand, and continue. It is a home for research preprints technical reports, project documentation, tutorials, and educational media created around the REC community.

The platform treats engineering as an iterative practice. A clear record of a failed PCB revision, a controls experiment, or a project handover can be as useful as a polished final result.

## Relationship to REC

OpenWorks is an initiative of VGU Robotics & Electronics Club. It supports the club's learning culture by giving members a durable, readable place to share methods and lessons. The records here are not issued by a university press, academic journal, or certification body.

## What it is not

REC OpenWorks is **not an academic journal**. Technical moderation checks presentation, rights, safety, and basic context; it is not formal academic peer review. A preprint remains a preprint unless its author documents a separate publication elsewhere.

Read the [platform policies]({{ '/policies/' | relative_url }}) for the operating principles behind this distinction.
