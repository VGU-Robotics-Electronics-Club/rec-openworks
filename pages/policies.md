## Content eligibility

Contributions should be relevant to engineering learning, research, or documentation and include enough context for a reader to understand their scope. Promotional, unsafe, unlawful, plagiarized, or deliberately misleading material is not eligible.

## Technical moderation

Moderators may request clarity, attribution, metadata, rights confirmation, or corrections before a record is listed. This process is a basic technical and editorial screen only. It is **not formal academic peer review** and does not certify the claims in a work.

## Copyright and intellectual property

Contributors retain copyright unless they choose a separate licence. They must have permission to share all included or linked material and must respect third-party licences, trademarks, and institutional rules.

## Confidentiality

Do not submit private submissions, reviewer reports, personal data, access credentials, confidential research, or proprietary material. Public records may be indexed by search engines and copied by others.

## Corrections and removal

Authors should report material errors promptly. REC may annotate, correct, unlist, or remove a record when there are factual, rights, safety, privacy, or policy concerns. Versions should state what changed.

## Preprint disclaimer

Records marked **Preprint — Not Peer Reviewed** are preliminary works. They must not be presented as peer-reviewed publications or formal academic certification. External publication status belongs to the author and the relevant publisher.
