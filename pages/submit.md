## What we can feature

- Research preprints indexed through clear metadata and a permitted external link.
- Engineering and technical reports.
- Project documentation, design notes, and reproducible build records.
- Tutorials, explanatory articles, and educational videos.

## Prepare the record

Provide a clear title, author names or team name, category, field, concise abstract, date, version, and relevant keywords. If you share an external repository, code repository, or video, make sure you have permission to link to it. Do not submit personal data, confidential material, unpublished work you are not allowed to share, or credentials.

## Technical moderation workflow

1. The contributor prepares a draft and checks rights and confidentiality.
2. A REC moderator checks scope, metadata, basic technical clarity, and public-sharing permission.
3. The contributor resolves any requested corrections.
4. Once cleared, a record can be published or indexed with its status stated plainly.

Moderation is not peer review, accreditation, endorsement, or a guarantee of technical correctness.

## Copyright and confidentiality

Authors retain their rights. Contributors are responsible for having permission to share text, images, code, and links. Do not send unpublished manuscripts, private review information, third-party secrets, or information covered by a confidentiality agreement.
